The Band

Formed in Liverpool in 1960, The Beatles are a legendary English band.
The band consisted of John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison and Ringo Starr.
The Beatles achieved 18 UK number 1 singles and 15 UK number 1 albums.
